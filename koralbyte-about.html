ABOUT KORALBYTE

Built on Big 4 rigor. Built for the businesses of Southeast Asia.

Koralbyte exists because too many ambitious companies are still run on spreadsheets that don't hold up and brands that don't stand out. We fix both.

WHO WE ARE

A group built around two disciplines.

Koralbyte is a Malaysia-headquartered group operating across Southeast Asia and North America, built around two disciplines: financial leadership and brand building.

Our advisory practice grew out of Big 4 training and more than a decade and a half of hands-on finance leadership inside real, high-growth companies: consumer brands scaling across DTC and retail, game studios shipping titles played by millions, and technology groups operating across multiple jurisdictions at once. That experience shaped how Koralbyte works: rigorous, evidence-led, and accountable at every level.

Alongside advisory, Koralbyte builds and owns its own ventures, platforms and studios where we apply the same standard to our own ideas that we bring to our clients' businesses.

OUR MISSION

Closing the infrastructure gap.

We believe that ambitious companies fail more often from weak infrastructure than from weak ideas. A brand without financial discipline cannot raise capital with confidence. A finance function without strategic backbone cannot support real growth.

Koralbyte's mission is to close that gap, on both sides. We give founders and operators the financial architecture to scale with control, and the brand and creative infrastructure to compete with companies twice their size. Our aim is not to hand over a deliverable and walk away. It is to become a long-term partner in how a company runs.

WHAT WE DO

Seven capabilities, one standard.

Fractional and Interim CFO Leadership

Financial strategy, board reporting, capital structure, and investor relations for companies that need C-suite finance leadership without a full-time hire.

Finance Function Build-Out

In-house finance team design, ERP implementation, close cycle optimization, and accounting policy setup for companies outgrowing outsourced or informal finance operations.

Capital Raise and Investor Readiness

Financial modeling, data room preparation, and due diligence support for companies preparing to raise equity or debt.

Accounting, Bookkeeping and Due Diligence

Ongoing financial operations and transaction due diligence across multiple jurisdictions and reporting standards.

Recruitment

Sourcing, assessment, and placement of finance and technology talent, including interim coverage during a search.

Company Incorporation and Compliance

Entity structuring, regulatory registration, and office location search and setup for companies entering or operating across Southeast Asian markets, including Kuala Lumpur and Jakarta.

Malaysia Digital and Talent Registrations

Malaysia Digital (MD) Status applications and MDEC liaison, incentive and benefits guidance, and Residence Pass Talent (RP-T) applications for eligible professionals.

Brand and Growth Creative

Naming, identity, pitch decks, e-commerce builds, and performance marketing through Koralbyte Studios, our in-house creative venture.

TRACK RECORD

Numbers that hold up under scrutiny.

Koralbyte's advisory work has supported over $29 million in equity and debt raises. Engagements have included cutting a monthly close cycle from 22 days to 7 in three months for a consumer brand, rebuilding a financial model from the ground up to support a $6 million raise, and reducing operating costs by 34 percent while shortening a close cycle from 25 to 9 days for a globally distributed games studio.

Today, Koralbyte manages financial strategy and reporting across 7 legal entities in 6 countries for a Yahoo Japan Group-backed company, covering board reporting, investor relations, and multi-entity treasury consolidation.

Koralbyte's practice is built on Big 4 training and rigor, shaped by audit and advisory work spanning government bodies, listed companies, international nonprofits, and major financial institutions. Our wider network of accounting and corporate services professionals is drawn from top-tier accounting firms, giving every engagement the same standard of technical grounding regardless of which part of the group is doing the work.

REGIONAL PRESENCE

Koralbyte is headquartered in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, with additional offices in Canada and Indonesia. We do not work at arm's length from the markets we serve. Where the work requires local knowledge of regulation, structuring, or market conditions, Koralbyte has it firsthand.
