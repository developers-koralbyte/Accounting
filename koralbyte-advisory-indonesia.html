🇮🇩 KORALBYTE ADVISORY · INDONESIA

Finance, tax, and corporate services in Indonesia.

Full-service advisory coverage in Indonesia, from company formation through ongoing accounting, tax, HR, and finance leadership. Every service below is delivered to the same Big 4-trained standard as the rest of the Koralbyte Group.

Market Entry

Foreign Investment Company (PT PMA)

The standard vehicle for foreign investors operating in Indonesia, giving you full control over the business without a local partner. We manage the full formation process, from name approval and BKPM licensing through to your OSS business identification number, and advise on minimum capital requirements as they apply to your sector.

  • Full formation from name approval to OSS registration
  • Guidance on the Negative Investment List and sector restrictions
  • Capital structuring advice
Learn more →

Local PT Company

For sectors closed or restricted to foreign ownership, a local PT company, formed through an Indonesian nominee shareholder arrangement, is the practical route to market. We structure this properly and legally, with the safeguards your business needs.

  • Local company formation and licensing
  • Nominee shareholder structuring
  • Compliance safeguards and documentation
Learn more →

Off-the-Shelf Company

A pre-registered, ready-to-operate company for businesses that need to start trading immediately rather than wait out a full incorporation timeline. We transfer ownership and handle the administrative handover so you can begin operating without delay.

  • Pre-incorporated companies available immediately
  • Ownership transfer and documentation
  • Fast route to market for time-sensitive launches
Learn more →

Representative Office

A non-trading presence for market research, liaison, and business development ahead of a full commercial launch. It's a lighter-weight way to establish a footprint in Indonesia before committing to a trading entity.

  • Market research and liaison entity
  • Lower setup and compliance burden than a trading company
  • Pathway to full incorporation later
Learn more →

Nominee Shareholder

A structured local shareholder arrangement that lets foreign investors participate in sectors with local ownership requirements, backed by proper legal documentation to protect your interests as the underlying investor.

  • Structured local shareholding arrangement
  • Legal documentation protecting investor interests
  • Used alongside local PT company formation
